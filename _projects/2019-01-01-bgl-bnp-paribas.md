[BGL-BNP Paribas](http://www.bgl.lu) is a leading bank of Luxembourg. We worked together to evaluate the robustness of their ML pipeline, in particular the themes of *Concept Drift*, *Generalization* and *Adversarial Attacks*.

We published together the paper: Search-based adversarial testing and improvement of constrained credit scoring systems, FSE2020 [preprint](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Search-based-adversarial-testing-and-improvement-of-Ghamizi-Cordy/32e8a4ac3a5609b961cf67b2aa2bec8a548e499c)
